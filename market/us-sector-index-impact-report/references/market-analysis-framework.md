# Market Analysis Framework

Use this checklist to keep the report specific and defensible.

## U.S. Data Collection

- Headline indices: Nasdaq Composite, Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 when useful, Dow only if relevant.
- Sector lens: S&P 500 sector indices, Nasdaq industry indexes, Philadelphia Semiconductor Index, biotech, software, banks, energy, materials, consumer discretionary, and small caps when they drive the session.
- Verify session status: normal close, half day, holiday, or source delay.
- Keep futures, premarket, and after-hours data separate from completed-session data.

## Cross-Market Channels

1. Risk appetite and liquidity
   - Strong U.S. tech usually lifts high-beta Hong Kong internet and A-share growth themes.
   - A broad U.S. selloff can pressure Asia through risk reduction even without direct earnings links.

2. Earnings and demand read-through
   - U.S. software/platform strength has limited direct read-through to China platforms unless advertising, cloud, or AI monetization evidence overlaps.
   - U.S. biotech strength can help Hong Kong biotech and A-share innovative drugs sentiment, but local approval and funding cycles matter.

3. Supply-chain and order read-through
   - Semiconductors, AI servers, optical modules, PCB, power equipment, and advanced packaging have the strongest China supply-chain read-through.
   - EV, solar, battery, and export-chain reads require checking U.S. demand, tariffs, commodity prices, and policy headlines.

## Core Factor and Fundamental Analysis

- Macro: Treasury yields, real rates, dollar index, oil, copper, credit spreads, and volatility. Explain whether price moves are discount-rate driven or earnings-revision driven.
- Global gold: label the instrument clearly and explain whether the move is driven by real rates, dollar weakness, geopolitical hedging, central-bank demand, inflation concern, or broad risk-off behavior.
- Earnings: revisions, guidance, margin commentary, backlog, order intake, inventory, pricing, and free cash flow.
- Valuation and positioning: whether the move looks like multiple expansion, short covering, defensive rotation, or fundamental upgrade.
- Policy and geopolitics: export controls, tariffs, China restrictions, fiscal incentives, energy policy, and antitrust headlines.
- Dollar policy: separate Fed policy expectations from official dollar rhetoric, Treasury/fiscal signals, liquidity conditions, and international spillovers into RMB, HKD, commodities, gold, and emerging-market risk appetite.
- Breadth: distinguish narrow megacap leadership from broad sector participation.

## U.S. AI Capital Expenditure Focus

- Hyperscaler capex: Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Oracle and other cloud/data-center operators. Track whether guidance implies acceleration, plateau, or digestion.
- Compute demand: GPUs, custom ASICs, HBM, foundry capacity, advanced packaging, AI servers, and rack-scale systems.
- Networking and infrastructure: optical modules, switches, CPO, copper interconnect, power equipment, cooling, data-center construction, and grid constraints.
- Fundamental tension: capex supports suppliers' revenue visibility but can pressure cloud/platform free cash flow, depreciation, return on invested capital, and investor tolerance for long payback periods.
- China read-through: strongest for A-share AI hardware, CPO, PCB, cooling, power, and semiconductor equipment/materials; more indirect for software and internet platforms.
- Risk signs: cloud capex guide-down, GPU lead-time normalization, order pushouts, HBM oversupply, power bottlenecks, rising depreciation, or monetization disappointment.

## Hong Kong Mapping

- U.S. megacap tech up: Hang Seng Tech and internet platforms get sentiment beta first.
- Semiconductors/AI hardware up: hardware, optical, components, and selected China AI infrastructure themes benefit; pure HK semiconductor exposure is narrower.
- Biotech up: HK innovative drug names can benefit if risk appetite and funding conditions improve.
- Energy/materials up: resource and commodity-linked HK names may respond more to commodity prices than equity-sector moves.
- Rates up or banks strong: insurers and banks may benefit; property and long-duration growth can lag.
- Dollar down and gold up: can support HK-listed gold miners, materials, and high-beta growth through liquidity, but geopolitical risk can still cap risk appetite.

## A-Share Mapping

- Semiconductors up: semiconductor equipment/materials, advanced packaging, chip design, AI servers, CPO, optical modules, PCB, cooling, and power supply.
- Nasdaq 100 up with narrow megacap leadership: AI application, cloud, software, media, and robotics sentiment may improve, but watch turnover breadth.
- Energy up: coal, oilfield services, petrochemicals; confirm crude and domestic policy.
- Materials up: nonferrous metals, chemicals, rare earths; confirm commodity prices and RMB trend.
- Gold up: map first to gold miners and precious metals, then to broader nonferrous only if copper/industrial metals confirm.
- Biotech up: innovative drugs, CRO/CDMO; local policy and financing remain key.
- Consumer discretionary up: export-chain, autos, home appliances, textiles; check tariffs and U.S. retail data.

## Report Tone

- Lead with the investable conclusion, not a market diary.
- Use "likely", "may", and "watch" for cross-market implications unless there is direct evidence.
- Separate high-conviction direct beneficiaries from sentiment-only beneficiaries.
- Include at least one contrary risk so the report does not read as one-way confirmation.
